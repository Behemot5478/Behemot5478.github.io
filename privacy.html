Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

OmniPulse ("we", "our", or "the Application") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is accessed, used, and protected when you use our creator management tools and connect your third-party social media accounts via official developer APIs.

2. Data Collection and Usage

OmniPulse operates primarily as a creator productivity tool. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

When you explicitly authorize OmniPulse through official developer OAuth flows, we access only the specific scopes you have approved:

  • user.info.profile: Used solely to identify the authenticated account, displaying your profile display name, avatar, verification status, and public profile link within your creator dashboard.
  • user.info.stats: Used to display aggregated creator metrics including follower counts, following counts, total video counts, and likes directly within your analytics dashboard.
  • video.list: Used to fetch and display your list of published videos so you can view content history and analyze video-level engagement.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens granted during OAuth authentication are stored securely and locally on the user's client environment. Tokens are never transmitted to unauthorized third parties or used for purposes outside the explicit creator dashboard functionality.

4. Revocation of Access

Users can revoke OmniPulse's access to their account at any time through their platform security and privacy settings or by disconnecting their account within the application.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@omnipulse.app or via our GitHub repository.
