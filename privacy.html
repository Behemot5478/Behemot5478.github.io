Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

1. Overview

TikTok Bot Studio is a creator productivity application that generates short-form videos and publishes them directly to authorized TikTok accounts using official TikTok APIs.

2. Data Collection & Privacy

We do not collect, sell, or share user personal data. OAuth tokens and video files are stored locally on the user's device.

3. TikTok API Permissions

We use user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload solely to upload and publish content on behalf of the creator.
